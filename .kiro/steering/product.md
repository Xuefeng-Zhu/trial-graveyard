# Product: Trial Graveyard

A Kiroween 2026 Costume Contest entry. It's a free-trial and subscription tracker that helps people cancel before they get charged. The spooky costume has to *carry the function*: urgency, status and savings are all communicated through a haunted graveyard.

## Core metaphor (keep it consistent)
- Each trial or subscription is a **grave** in the graveyard.
- As the next charge date gets closer, the grave **cracks open** in stages: >7 days (sealed), 7–3 days (cracked), 3–2 days (earth churning, faint green glow).
- Within **48 hours** of the charge, the grave **rises as a zombie** that shambles toward the front of the screen. This is the call to action.
- **Cancelled** trials become a tombstone with an epitaph and the money saved: "Here lies Necro Audiobooks. Saved you $14.95/mo." They rest in peace.
- **Kept** (paid) subscriptions are **ghosts** that drift above the graveyard, showing their monthly cost.
- The **Mausoleum** keeps a running total of money saved (the "souls saved" counter).

## Users
People who start free trials and forget to cancel them, like deal hunters and busy parents. The demo persona has around 8 trials and subscriptions.

## Must-haves
- Add a trial in under 10 seconds: name, charge date, price, cadence, and a cancel URL.
- Paste a receipt or confirmation email to auto-fill the fields (parsing).
- Every grave links to "Cancel now" and offers "I cancelled" and "Keep it" actions.
- An optional reminder via a calendar file (.ics) export for charge dates.
- Fully accessible: a "Daylight mode" toggle switches to a calm list view, and the app respects prefers-reduced-motion and keyboard navigation. Spooky must never block usability.
- Works offline and is local-first. No account is needed for the demo.

## Tone
Darkly playful copy with puns. Never mean to the user.
